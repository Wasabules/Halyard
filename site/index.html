What it does

Your cloud machine, on a console that fits in a bag.

Sign in with your Shadow account, pick your machine, play. Halyard speaks the same protocol as Shadow's desktop client, reimplemented from scratch for homebrew.

  • Hardware video decoding

    H.264 and HEVC on Switch, H.264 on Vita, drawn with the GPU's own colour conversion.

  • Opus or FLAC audio

    Whichever the server grants, with a 5-band equaliser and profiles that follow the console's mode.

  • Your controls reach the VM

    Gamepad, a mouse driven from the sticks or the touchscreen, an on-screen keyboard. Rumble and gyro aiming on Switch.

  • The real pointer

    The VM's own mouse cursor is drawn, rather than a local approximation.

  • It measures itself

    Latency by stage at p50/p90/p99, packet loss and the link's estimated capacity — in the pause menu, while you play.

  • Settings that stay

    Testers for the network, the pad and the mouse, and an optional lock on opening the app.

Install

Three steps, on a console that already runs homebrew

Nintendo Switch

Needs Atmosphère and the homebrew menu.

  1. Download halyard.nro.
  2. Copy it to /switch/ on the SD card.
  3. Open the homebrew menu and launch Halyard.

PS Vita / PS TV

Needs HENkaku or h-encore, and VitaShell.

  1. Download halyard.vpk.
  2. Copy it to the console and install it from VitaShell.
  3. Launch Halyard from LiveArea.

The first launch shows a short code: type it at the address shown, on a phone or a computer, and the console signs in by itself. Details in the install guide.

Before you play

What to expect

Your network matters more than the console. The software spends a few milliseconds on a frame; a Wi-Fi stall lasts hundreds. A wired link, where you can have one, changes more than any setting.

Cap the bitrate around 25 Mb/s. Above that, the picture breaks up in play over Wi-Fi. The setting is in the pause menu — hold + on Switch or START on Vita.

You need your own Shadow subscription. Halyard is a client: it signs in as you, to your machine.

Unofficial, and at your own risk

Halyard is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Shadow, Nintendo, or Sony Interactive Entertainment. Those names appear here only to say which service it talks to and which hardware it runs on.
